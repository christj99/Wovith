# Stage 1 Validation Plan

## Goal

Validate whether Wovith is useful as a private-alpha daily work lens without adding connector scope beyond Google Calendar events.

## Seven-Day Plan

For seven days:

1. Open Wovith once near the start of the work day.
2. Use at least one Stage 1 lens.
3. Record one useful signal if one appears.
4. Record one noisy or missing signal if one appears.
5. Inspect at least one Why panel.
6. Edit, duplicate, disable, or delete at least one cell during the week.
7. Record any privacy or permission concerns.

Use `STAGE_1_ALPHA_LOG_TEMPLATE.md`.

## Success Criteria

- Wovith is opened at least 5 of 7 days.
- At least one useful signal is found per active day.
- The user can explain lens and cell behavior.
- Why is inspected at least once per session.
- At least one cell or lens is edited during the week.
- There are no privacy surprises.

## Private Data Warning

Do not commit event titles, descriptions, locations, attendees, links, or screenshots containing private calendar data.

## Scope Boundary

This validation uses the existing read-only Google Calendar connector only. It does not validate Gmail, Drive, Tasks, writes, sync, mobile, MCP, NL/model integration, or production OAuth verification.
